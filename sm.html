Misandry is systematic and perpetrated by institutions in the same manner as misogyny or sometimes even worse as is evident from the fact that it is not acknowledged or done away with, even in comparatively progressive societies as of today. The word ‘systematic’ in this context refers to prejudice, discrimination against a particular group and lack of equal opportunity and benefit that is offered to a particular demography, perpetrated in an automatic manner via policies, laws and stereotypes that shape the said societies beyond an individual’s actions. Another principle of this word states that this is perpetrated by individuals belonging to another demography who are in position of power in society. The statement that misandry is somehow not as bad as misogyny because the former is not systematic, is a blatant lie propagated by feminists. Below is a comprehensive list of all the issues which discriminate against men(Misandry) and are perpetrated by institutions of law, organizations with societal power and social stereotypes(Systematic) discussed in an overall manner. Although each point can be elaborated in detail, I will just discuss that portion of the problem which makes it a systematic issue.
